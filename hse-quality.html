Health, Safety, Environment (HSE) & Quality Assurance

At NKEM GLOBAL SERVICES LIMITED, zero injuries, zero property damage, and zero environmental incidents are fundamental operational baselines. We consider HSE compliance a key production metric; an unsafe operation cannot be productive.

Non-Negotiable Field Empowerment

The Stop-Work Authority (SWA) Culture

Every employee and deployed craftsperson from NGS is trained and empowered with full Stop-Work Authority.

“If a worker spots an unaddressed hazard, unstable scaffolding, an uncertified lifting sling, or an unmonitored gas risk, they are required to stop the job immediately without fear of penalty. Work only resumes when the area is made safe and certified by the supervising authority.”

This culture ensures that our personnel never take shortcuts, bypass client permits, or compromise international standards to meet arbitrary speed targets.

100% Fall-Arrest Compliance

Field HSE Enforcement & Safety Harnesses

Technicians working at height with certified full-body fall-arrest harnesses, scaffold tags, and personal protective gear — zero tolerance for unsafe work fronts.

Operational Enforcement

Health, Safety & Environmental (HSE) Policy Pillars

How our five operational pillars translate into daily enforcement across complex oil, gas, petrochemical, and construction facilities.

HSE Pillar Operational Execution & Enforcement
1. Pre-Job Hazard Analysis JSA Protocol Before starting any task, our supervisors review the site JSA with the full crew, identifying pinch points, pressurized lines, drop hazards, and environmental risks.
2. PPE Compliance Standards Certified Gear Every deployed worker is equipped with standard PPE: flame-retardant coveralls, impact gloves, steel-toed boots, eye protection, and hard hats. Specialised task-specific PPE (respirators, harnesses) is inspected daily.
3. Heat Stress & Fatigue Control Worker Welfare Structured hydration stations, scheduled shade rests during high heat index hours, and shift rotations to ensure workers remain alert on critical jobs.
4. Environmental Protection Zero Spills Immediate deployment of drip trays under stationary power units and fuel transfer points. Hydrocarbon spills are contained using certified absorbent materials and reported without delay.
5. Incident Reporting Daily Safety Huddles All near-misses and unsafe conditions are formally documented and addressed at daily safety huddles, preventing repeat occurrences.
Site Discipline

Worksite Ethics, Culture & Operating Standards

Technical capability alone does not guarantee a successful project; worksite culture determines outcomes. At NGS, we maintain a firm code of field discipline. When our personnel put on client or NGS-branded coveralls, they represent our collective commitment to safety, professionalism, and respect for site authority.

Operating Standard Field Implementation & Enforcement
Site Discipline & Punctuality Pre-Shift Muster Crews arrive 30 minutes before shift commencement for medical gate checks, equipment muster, and mandatory toolbox briefings. Zero tolerance for unauthorised absences or insubordination.
Permit-to-Work (PTW) Compliance Mandatory JSA No NGS personnel may touch a tool, enter a confined vessel, or start a hot-work activity without a verified, signed client Permit to Work (PTW) and approved Job Safety Analysis (JSA).
Substance Abuse Prohibitions Zero Tolerance Strict zero-tolerance policy regarding alcohol and unprescribed drugs. Random testing is conducted, and immediate dismissal follows any violation.
Client Asset Protection Tool Logging Careful handling of client machinery, materials, electrical systems, and tools. Systematic tool logging at shift start and close prevents loss, tool damage, or foreign-object contamination.
Interpersonal Respect Multinational Respect Professional interaction with multinational site managers, site engineers, QA/QC inspectors, and third-party contractors regardless of nationality or background.

Supervisory Integrity

Dedicated Field Supervisory Structure

On all substantial projects, NGS provides dedicated non-working site supervisors. They bridge communication between international client managers and local labour, manage site attendance, coordinate task permits, and resolve community-related matters before work is disrupted.

  • No Productive Diversion: Supervisors do not perform manual trades; their sole responsibility is safety compliance, attendance, and quality control.
  • Linguistic & Cultural Bridge: Fluent coordination with expatriate EPC directors and local craft hands.
  • Permit & JSA Custodians: Daily management of Permit-to-Work documents, gas testing logs, and shift cards.
