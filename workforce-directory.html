Candidate Onboarding & Testing Protocol

How NGS verifies technical competencies before any tradesperson enters a client site gate.

1

Practical Coupon Test

Welders run practical test coupons (radiographic & ultrasonic X-ray verification) under certified welding inspectors.

2

Medical Fitness

Pre-employment medical clearance including audiometry, chest X-rays, visual acuity, and certified drug/alcohol screens.

3

Safety Induction

Mandatory orientation on Stop-Work Authority (SWA), client Permit-to-Work rules, and hazard identification.

4

Badging & Dispatch

Client site security badging, PPE issuance, and supervised transport to work zone under working foremen.
