Mobilisation & Project Delivery Workflow

Delivering consistent support requires an orderly, repeatable process. From initial scope review to final close-out documentation, NGS adheres to a six-stage execution protocol.

01 Stage 01

Understand

Scope Clarification & Site Survey

Clarify craft trade levels, cleaning areas, site hazards, shift patterns, PPE standards, and project schedule milestones.

02 Stage 02

Plan

Resource & Safety Planning

Develop task method statements, Job Safety Analyses (JSA), tool allocation rosters, and host community labour distribution plans.

03 Stage 03

Mobilise

Vetting, Inductions & Logistics

Conduct medical clearance, safety briefings, client security badging, and secure transport deployment to the operational work zone.

04 Stage 04

Execute

Deploy crews under working foremen; manage daily permits, tool checks, shift muster, and direct non-working field supervision.

05 Stage 05

Verify

Joint Inspection & QA Sign-off

Conduct walkthroughs with client QA/QC inspectors, obtain signed daily shift cards, and resolve punch-list items immediately.

06 Stage 06

Close-out

Demobilisation & Reporting

Compile verified timesheets, complete safe demobilisation handovers, and submit final invoices with transparent audit backups.

Zero-Tolerance Safety Protocol

The Stop-Work Authority (SWA) Culture

Every employee and deployed craftsperson from NGS is trained and empowered with full Stop-Work Authority. If a worker spots an unaddressed hazard, unstable scaffolding, an uncertified lifting sling, or an unmonitored gas risk, they are required to stop the job immediately without fear of penalty. Work only resumes when the area is made safe and certified.

Review Full HSE Policy →

5 Core HSE Pillars Enforced Daily:

  • 1. Pre-Job Hazard Analysis: Daily JSA review with entire crew covering pinch points & pressurized lines.
  • 2. PPE Standards: Flame-retardant coveralls, impact gloves, steel boots, hard hats & harness checks.
  • 3. Heat Stress & Fatigue: Hydration stations, scheduled shade rests, and alert shift rotations.
  • 4. Environmental Protection: Drip trays under power units, certified absorbents for spill containment.
  • 5. Incident Reporting: Near-misses documented and reviewed at daily safety huddles.
