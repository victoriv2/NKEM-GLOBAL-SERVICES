Executive Overview & Positioning

Bridging Large-Scale Engineering with Dependable Local Execution

NKEM GLOBAL SERVICES LIMITED (NGS) is an indigenous Nigerian service provider established to bridge the critical operational gap between large-scale engineering contractors and dependable local site execution.

Operating from our administrative and logistics base in Oyigbo, Rivers State, NGS supplies the vital industrial labour force, engineering trades, and daily project support required to execute major oil and gas, power generation, petrochemical, and heavy infrastructure projects safely and strictly on schedule.

Our operational philosophy is built on clear, practical field realities rather than abstract corporate claims. We understand that international EPC leaders — including Turkish, Italian, Korean, Indian, and multinational joint-venture consortia — require local partners who understand international safety standards, respect rigid inspection schedules, and maintain total control over workforce discipline, medical readiness, and community stability.

Zero-Tolerance Safety Protocol

The Stop-Work Authority (SWA) Culture

Every employee and deployed craftsperson from NGS is trained and empowered with full Stop-Work Authority. If a worker spots an unaddressed hazard, unstable scaffolding, an uncertified lifting sling, or an unmonitored gas risk, they are required to stop the job immediately without fear of penalty. Work only resumes when the area is made safe and certified.

Review Full HSE Policy →

5 Core HSE Pillars Enforced Daily:

  • 1. Pre-Job Hazard Analysis: Daily JSA review with entire crew covering pinch points & pressurized lines.
  • 2. PPE Standards: Flame-retardant coveralls, impact gloves, steel boots, hard hats & harness checks.
  • 3. Heat Stress & Fatigue: Hydration stations, scheduled shade rests, and alert shift rotations.
  • 4. Environmental Protection: Drip trays under power units, certified absorbents for spill containment.
  • 5. Incident Reporting: Near-misses documented and reviewed at daily safety huddles.
