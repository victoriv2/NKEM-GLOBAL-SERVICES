Core Service 03

Manpower & Workforce Solutions

Sourcing, screening, mobilising, and managing skilled labour in industrial environments is a major challenge for project managers. NGS takes full ownership of workforce management, providing reliable talent matched to specific task scopes.

Full Ownership of Technical Workforce Operations

When international contractors enter Nigeria, unexpected workforce friction can derail project schedules within weeks. NGS removes this risk completely. We maintain an active, screened database of over 250 skilled trades ready for rapid mobilisation within 48 to 72 hours.

Every single craftsperson deployed under NGS is tested for technical ability, medically certified for heavy industrial fitness, and managed under our dedicated on-site supervisory structure.

Quality Gatekeepers

Vetting and Administration Workflow

Three sequential vetting gates guarantee that no unverified or medically unfit personnel enter your site.

Trade Skill Verification

Welders and fitters undergo practical workshop test coupons (radiographic/ultrasonic testing) before being cleared for client deployment. Only verified trades receive dispatch authorisation.

Medical Fitness Screening

Standard industrial pre-employment medical examinations (audiometry, chest X-rays, visual acuity, drug/alcohol screening) conducted through certified occupational health clinics.

Turnkey Payroll & Statutory Cover

NGS manages daily payroll, host-community remittances, PAYE tax, Employee Compensation Act (NSITF) cover, and group personal accident policies, shielding clients from legal liabilities.

Core Service 04

Project Support & Cleaning Services

On high-risk industrial sites, cleaning is not simply cosmetic — it is an essential safety and engineering requirement. Debris-strewn fabrication shops, oil-contaminated decks, blocked drainage trenches, and uncleaned process equipment cause slip-and-trip hazards, increase fire risks, and delay project commissioning. NGS provides structured industrial cleaning and logistical field support.

Storage Tank Work

Tank Surface Prep & Cleaning at Height

Surface preparation and industrial cleaning at height on a bulk storage tank prior to protective coating applications.

Skid Support

Field Piping & Valve Maintenance Support

Assisting mechanical crews with equipment skid support, valve maintenance, flange cleaning, and hydro-testing preparation.

Industrial Cleaning Work Packages

Turnaround & Shutdown Cleaning

High-pressure washing and mechanical scraping of heat exchangers, process vessels, storage tanks, reboilers, and sumps during planned turnaround maintenance.

Post-Construction Clean-down

Comprehensive clearing of construction debris, spent electrodes, structural cut-offs, insulation scraps, and paint drums ahead of pre-commissioning walkthroughs.

Shop & Yard Housekeeping

Continuous shift-by-shift removal of grinding swarf, blast grit, metal off-cuts, and grease spills to maintain safe, hazard-free fabrication work zones.

Drainage & Trench Clearance

Manual and mechanical clearing of industrial storm drains, oily-water separators, containment bunds, and culverts across facility plots.

Facility & Office Janitorial

Routine daily and deep sanitisation for project office complexes, site clinics, muster shelters, meeting rooms, and camp dormitories.

Rapid Mobilisation

All cleaning crews deploy fully equipped with explosion-proof pneumatic tools, pressure washers, and certified respiratory gear.
